## Codebase Patterns

- **A commit can land without its bookkeeping.** An iteration that is cut off after
  step 6 leaves the implementation on the branch with `passes:false` everywhere. The
  next iteration's first move is `git log --oneline -5` against the PRD: if HEAD is
  already `feat: [US-x] - <that story's title>`, the work exists — RE-VERIFY it and
  close the books, do not re-implement it. Two implementations of one story on one
  branch is the expensive failure.
- **`shellcheck` is not installed on this host.** `bash -n` over `bin/chief
  engine/*.sh test/*.sh install.sh` is the local half; CI (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`)
  is the enforcing half. Say which one you ran in `notes` rather than claiming both.
- **A new `test/*.sh` needs THREE lists** (already noted in CLAUDE.md, confirmed here):
  `.chief/verify.sh`'s `CHIEF_BYSTANDER_TESTS`, `test/all.sh`'s `BASH_SUITE`, and
  `.github/workflows/ci.yml`. `terminal-false` is in all three.

## US-1 — A story can declare that FALSE is its terminal answer

The implementation landed in `be444da` on a prior iteration that never reached steps
7–9, so this iteration re-verified it end to end and recorded it.

**What is there.** `engine/terminal.sh` — a new module holding the whole vocabulary:
`TERMINAL_FIELD='terminalFalse'`, and one jq prelude (`TERMINAL_JQ`) defining
`declared` / `attempted` / `negative` / `inert` / `settled`. **SETTLED** is now the
predicate the engine counts completion from — `passes:true` OR (declared + a recorded
measurement) — and `passes` is never rewritten, because the answer really is false and
that IS the finding.

Files: `engine/terminal.sh` (new), `engine/driver.sh` (sources it at :483; remaining
is OPEN not unpassed at the completion sites; `terminal_say_inert` /
`terminal_say_negative` at the stops), `engine/agent.sh` (SETTLED drives `_passes` /
`_story` / `_passed_ids` and the prompt's ALREADY-DONE roster), `engine/lib.sh` (the
`completed/` record leaves a `terminalFalse` story's `passes` alone), `bin/chief`
(`lint` type-checks the boolean; `ps` distinguishes the two), `engine/measure.sh`,
`engine/monitor.sh`, `docs/reference/tasklist-schema.md`, `test/terminal-false.sh`
(new, wired into all three gate lists), `VERSION` 0.8.98 + README.

**Two things worth knowing.**
- `observed` is **shared** with `engine/measure.sh`, not copied — the inert rule and
  the BAR rule ask the same question, and a second copy would drift into two answers.
- `prd_state_source` was **moved** out of `driver.sh` into `terminal.sh`. It is
  unrelated to terminal-false and still reads `$name`/`$branch`/`$sub`/`$SNAP`/`$SRC`
  by dynamic scope from the driver. It works (smoke + the resume tests are green), but
  it is misfiled — if a later story touches that area, `driver.sh` is where a reader
  will look for it.

**Observed.** `test/terminal-false.sh` PASS, including the corpus assertion — 40
existing tasklists classify identically before and after. `bash -n` clean over
`bin/chief engine/*.sh test/*.sh install.sh`. `smoke` PASS · `ratelimit` PASS ·
`noworkguard` PASS · `measured-bars` PASS · `evidence-gate` PASS · `version-bump` PASS
(VERSION 0.8.98 covers every engine/bin change on HEAD) · `doc-sync` PASS.
`shellcheck` is absent on this host; CI enforces it.

**Learnings for future iterations.**
- US-2 (stall detection) and US-3 (`chief retire --negative`) both build on
  `terminal.sh`. Reuse `TERMINAL_JQ`'s five `def`s — do not re-spell "settled".
- US-3's command is already named in `terminal.sh`'s header comment as
  `chief retire --negative`; `bin/chief` does not have it yet. Adding a subcommand
  re-stales `doc-sync` (README's command table) and `version-bump` (VERSION) together.
- A backtick in an unquoted heredoc RUNS — `bin/chief`'s usage blocks are `cat <<EOF`.
  Escape them `\`` when documenting the new flag.
